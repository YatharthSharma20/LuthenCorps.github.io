Occupations

Browse work entries by occupation.

  • Filmmaker (1)
    Plans, directs, and oversees the production of films — coordinating crew, managing shoots, and making creative and logistical decisions throughout production.
  • Data Engineer (1)
    Builds and maintains the systems that collect, store, and process data — pipelines, databases, and infrastructure that other teams depend on for analytics and decision-making.
  • Structural Engineer (1)
    Analyzes and designs structures to ensure they can safely support the loads they will carry — calculating forces, sizing structural members, and producing engineering drawings.
  • Data Scientist
    Investigates data to find patterns, build models, and answer questions — writing code, running experiments, and communicating findings to inform decisions.
  • Software Engineer
    Designs, writes, tests, and maintains software — solving technical problems, debugging issues, and building systems that users and other engineers depend on.
  • Protection and Controls Engineer (1)
    Designs and verifies protection and control systems for power substations — ensuring breakers, relays, and associated wiring respond correctly during faults to keep the grid safe.
