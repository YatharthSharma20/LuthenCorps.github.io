Making of Django Unchained — Behind the Scenes

The Problem

Creating the visual world of a period Western set in the pre-Civil War American South, including set design, costume work, location scouting, and coordinating complex action sequences.

What Happened

A behind-the-scenes look at the production of Django Unchained, showing the actual work involved in filmmaking — from building sets and designing costumes to coordinating stunts and shooting on location. The footage shows how crew members solve practical problems on set and the many collaborative decisions that go into producing a major film.

The Actual Work

  1. Location scouting and selecting shooting sites that match the period setting.
  2. Building and dressing sets to create believable 1850s environments.
  3. Designing and fitting costumes that are historically informed but serve the story.
  4. Coordinating action sequences and stunt work with safety considerations.
  5. Managing continuity across shooting days and multiple locations.
  6. Working through weather, lighting, and other on-set challenges.

Result

A completed feature film that required coordinating hundreds of crew members across multiple departments, each solving their own set of practical and creative problems.
