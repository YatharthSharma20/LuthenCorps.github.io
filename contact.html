FAQs

Quick Answers

How much does it cost to work with LuthenCorps? +
Our fee is performance-based — we take 15–20% of the total sales revenue generated through your affiliate program (negotiable based on program size). You only pay when sales actually happen. No upfront fees, no retainers.
How long does it take to get my program live? +
We aim to have your affiliate program set up and live on Impact.com within 7 days of onboarding. Affiliate recruitment and first results typically follow within the first 2–4 weeks.
I'm an affiliate — how do I join? +
Just drop us a message through WhatsApp or the form above. Tell us about your audience, niche, and what kind of brands you'd like to promote. We'll match you with the right opportunities.
What commission do affiliates earn? +
Affiliates earn up to 20% commission on every confirmed sale they drive. The exact rate depends on the brand and product category. All commissions are tracked transparently through Impact.com.
Are there any fixed costs? +
The only fixed cost is Impact.com's platform fee of $30/month (~₹2,500) — paid directly to Impact.com, not to us. Everything else is performance-based: our management fee (15–20%) and affiliate commissions (up to 20%) are only charged when actual sales happen. No sales, no extra cost.
What kind of brands do you work with? +
We work with D2C brands, e-commerce businesses, SaaS products, and any brand selling online that wants to grow through performance-based affiliate marketing. If you sell online, we can help.
